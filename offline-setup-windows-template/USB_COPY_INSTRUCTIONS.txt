USB copy instructions (Windows) - beginner friendly

On the online computer (where you downloaded the files):
1) Plug in a USB drive (recommended: 32 GB or larger, USB 3.0).
2) Open File Explorer and find the USB drive letter (for example E:).
3) Right-click the 'offline-setup-windows-template' folder on your Desktop and choose "Send to" → [Your USB drive].
4) Wait until the copy finishes (the progress bar disappears).
5) Click the small USB icon in the system tray (bottom-right) and choose "Eject [USB name]".
6) When Windows says it’s safe, remove the USB and bring it to the offline computer.

On the offline computer (first steps):
1) Insert the USB and copy the 'offline-setup-windows-template' folder to the Desktop.
2) BEFORE running anything, turn off Wi‑Fi and unplug any Ethernet cables.
3) Then open the 'installers' folder and run the LM Studio and Jan installers.
